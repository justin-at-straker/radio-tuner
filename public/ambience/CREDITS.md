# Ambience credits

- `fireplace.opus`: "Cozy indoor fireplace ambience" by GFX Sounds. Re-encoded to Opus and loudness-normalised; looped with a crossfade in the app.
- `pages/turn-1.opus`: "Turning a page" by planish, 2007, public domain, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turning_a_page.ogg).
- `pages/turn-2.opus` to `pages/turn-5.opus`: cut from "Book paper pages assorted" by stephan, 2007, public domain, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Book_paper_pages_assorted.ogg).
- `typewriter/*.opus`: twelve keystrokes, two space bars, the margin bell and the carriage return, cut from "WWS Typewriter" by Work With Sounds / Konrad Gutkowski, 2015, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:WWS_Typewriter.ogg). Trimmed, faded and levelled.
